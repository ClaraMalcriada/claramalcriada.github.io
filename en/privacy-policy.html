Privacy Policy

How this site collects, uses and protects your data — in plain English.

This policy explains how the Clara Malcriada website collects, uses and protects visitors' personal data.

1. Data we collect

We collect only the email address you type into the newsletter form. The form is processed by Formspree, a service that receives and forwards the message — no other personal data is requested or stored by this site.

2. How we use data

Email addresses are used exclusively to send the newsletter (news, new releases and promotions). We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, except Formspree, which acts only as the technical processor of the form.

3. Consent

By subscribing to the newsletter, you agree to the storage and use of your email for the purposes described above. You can unsubscribe at any time via the unsubscribe link included in every email, or by writing to the official contact channel.

4. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time — just send an email to the contact channel listed on this site. We answer requests within 30 days.

5. Affiliate links

This site participates in the Amazon affiliate program. Purchase links may record browsing data (such as cookies) for sales attribution, without identifying who you are. Amazon has its own privacy policy, which governs everything that happens there.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in our practices. Significant changes will be announced on this same page and, when relevant, by email to newsletter subscribers.
