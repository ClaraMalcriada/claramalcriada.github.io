Who is Clara Malcriada?

Clara Malcriada was born somewhere between a botched scolding and a comeback delivered behind someone's back. She showed a gift for saying what she thinks from an early age — especially when nobody asked. At 7, she said "I don't have to" for the first time, and never stopped.

With a degree in Applied Sarcasm Arts and a specialization in Passive-Aggressive Communication, Clara built a career turning everyday situations into verbal weapons of moderate (emotional) destruction. She rose to fame after being kicked out of three family WhatsApp groups — with honors and zero regrets.
