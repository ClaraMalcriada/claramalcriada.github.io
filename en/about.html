About Clara Malcriada

Brazilian author of sarcastic books on insults, irony and elegant verbal comebacks.

Clara Malcriada is a Brazilian author of sarcastic books on insults, irony and elegant verbal comebacks. Her work explores how different cultures — from imperial China to the Russia of the tsars, from the Arab world to the Persia of the poets — turned criticism into a literary genre. All titles are available on Amazon in Kindle format, and most are free to read with a Kindle Unlimited subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Clara Malcriada?

Clara Malcriada is a Brazilian author of sarcastic books on insults, irony and elegant verbal comebacks, published in Kindle format on Amazon. She is the creator of The Art of Insults series.

Where can I buy Clara Malcriada's books?

All titles are available on Amazon in Kindle format, and most are included in the Kindle Unlimited program. Buy links are on this page and on the site's home page.

What is the reading order of The Art of Insults series?

The volumes (Chinese Indirection, Russian Insults, Arabic Insults and Persian Insult) are independent and can be read in any order. Suggested order: Chinese, Russian, Arabic, Persian.

Are the books available in English?

Yes. The main titles are published in English editions on Amazon.com, in addition to the original Brazilian Portuguese editions.

Do I need a Kindle device to read the books?

No. Any Kindle e-reader or the free Kindle app for phone, tablet or computer works. With a Kindle Unlimited subscription, most titles can be read at no extra cost.
