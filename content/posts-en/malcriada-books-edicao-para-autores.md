Some people write. Others want to write but get stuck in the boring part: formatting, picking keywords, uploading the file to Amazon, waiting for review and trying to understand the reports. Malcriada Books is for that second part.

We do not write your book for you. You bring the text, we take it to the shelf.

## What we do

We read the manuscript and point out what needs fixing. Then come line editing and chapter structure, followed by the ebook and the hardcover. The cover is prepared at the right size for the chosen paper, with the spine calculated from the real page count.

For publication, we put together title, subtitle, description, categories and keywords. The KDP upload and submission for review are part of the job too. Once the book is live, we track KENP and sales so we can adjust the positioning.

## How it works in practice

The first step is a simple conversation: what the book is about, which language it will be published in and where you want to sell it. From there we agree on a scope and a deadline. We do not promise sales miracles. We promise the right file, in the right format, with solid metadata.

If you already have a finished book and like what you see here, the door is open. Use the contact form and tell us a bit about the project.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you write the book?

No. The text is yours. We work on what you already wrote: editing, formatting, cover, metadata and publishing.

### Which languages do you publish in?

Portuguese and English. The language sets the store, the metadata and the keyword strategy.

### How long does it take?

It depends on the state of the manuscript and the queue. After the first conversation, you get a scope with an agreed deadline.
